HomeOwner Foundation is a non governmental organization dedicated to providing safe, affordable hommes for the hommeless families across Nigeria. Together, we are turning the dream of home ownership into reality-one family at a time.


Welcome Message from MD

Chief Emeka Okafor

Managing Director, Homeowner Foundation

Our mission is simple yet transformative: to eradicate homelessness in Nigeria by providing affordable, quality housing to families who need it most. Since our inception, we have built over 500 homes across 12 states, impacting more than 3000 lives.

We work hand-in-hand with local communities, government agencies, and generous donors to ensure every home we build is not just a shelter but a stepping stone to a brighter future. Together, we can end homelessness in our lifetime.
